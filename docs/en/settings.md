# Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Tab Title | The label under which this column appears as a tab. |

This is the only setting. Everything else is determined by the arrangement
of the columns.

## The “Name Tab” Window

When you open the widget’s settings, the
**Name Tab** window appears in full-screen mode:

- **On the left** are the **tabs in the section**, listed by number and column; the
  tab you are currently editing is highlighted. The list is for
  viewing only—you can change the number and order in Studio within the section.
- **In the center**, at the top, is “Tab 2 of 4” and the column; below that is the
  **Tab Title** field and a small preview labeled **This is how the
  bar appears**, which updates as you type.
- **Apply** saves the title to the widget’s settings;
  **Cancel** discards it. Changes are only saved by clicking **Save** in
  Studio. Undo, Redo, and Help (F1) are located at the top of the
  header.

If it’s impossible to determine which section the tab belongs to—for example, because
another tab has the exact same name—the left side displays a note instead of
the list. If the block isn’t in any column of a section, it warns you: This
means it won’t become a tab on the page.

The **Name Tab** button in the
settings reopens a closed window.

## Notes on the Title

- **Keep it short.** The labels appear side by side on a single line;
  long titles push the other tabs out of the visible area.
- **If left blank**, `Tab 1`, `Tab 2`, etc., will appear on the page instead.
  In the editor, the label will then read `Tab: still untitled`. This isn’t an error,
  but it looks like one to readers—so always set a title.
- The title is captured by the page’s **automatic translation** and
  appears to readers in their language. If a translation is left blank,
  the original title is displayed.

## What You Cannot Configure

The number, order, and grouping of the tabs are not configurable;
instead, they follow the column layout (see “Step by Step”). The appearance,
colors, and position of the tab bar are fixed.
